The answer in one minute

What you can say—without overstating it.

SET Loop connects an established retail operating pattern—governed intake, localized direction, role-based execution, evidence, feedback, and reporting—using the tools and files already present in the workflow. Production access, enterprise automation, advanced AI, and savings remain explicit validation gates.

Leadership-safe points

  • The market pattern is established. Retail platforms document localized planning, task execution, audits, proof, and reporting.
  • The pilot is working and bounded. It demonstrates role views, intake, checklists, feedback, referral links, exports, and source-aware Q&A in the browser.
  • The differentiator is the connected thread. SET Loop links upstream campaign/asset identity to field execution and learning.
  • Deterministic first; AI second. Approved sources, exact IDs, visible exceptions, and accountable owners come before assistance.

Language to use

Working pilot means the current experience performs the stated browser-local behavior.

Verify entitlement means the vendor capability exists, while plan, permission, policy, environment, and support still require owner confirmation.

Not assumed means the capability is outside the current promise.

“Reuse-first” is the strategy.
“Already licensed” is not the claim.

SET LOOP / AI CONTROLCAPABILITY + LICENSE BRIEF
Grounded assistance

Useful because it knows when not to answer.

Approved sources only.

Brand Guides, accepted promotion manifests, connected partner records, and role-permitted operational data.

The SET Agent may

  • Locate approved direction and cite the source.
  • Explain a placement, pack, task, or readiness record already present.
  • Sort and summarize role-permitted information.
  • Ask the user to refine an ambiguous question.
  • Prepare an escalation with context and the accountable partner copied.

The SET Agent may not

  • Search the public web for operational answers.
  • Invent placement, quantity, offer, tier, inventory, timing, or approval.
  • Treat a visual similarity as approved store applicability.
  • Expose content the user is not permitted to access.
  • Submit an order, approve a release, or change a source system without an authorized human action.
Production gate

Governance required

Identity, permissions, retrieval scope, citations, logging, prompt-injection controls, data-loss prevention, evaluation, human approval, and support ownership.

SET LOOP / CLAIM CONTROLCAPABILITY + LICENSE BRIEF
What stays outside the pitch

Boundaries make the story stronger.

Do not claim

  • Confirmed license savings, zero incremental cost, or universal access.
  • Feature parity with Blue Yonder, RELEX, Zebra, YOOBIC, or any other platform.
  • Automatic image recognition, photo scoring, digital twins, optimization, or live inventory.
  • Production SharePoint, Power Apps, Power Automate, AEM, UXP, or AI deployment before approval.
  • Central tracking from the public/browser-local prototype.
  • Automatic VMH upload or order placement.

Leadership decision frame

Build first

Deterministic intake, metadata, role views, checklists, source lineage, exports, feedback, and QA.

Connect next

Approved SharePoint, promotion, asset, Blue Yonder, time, capacity, inventory, and identity sources.

Buy or extend when justified

Computer vision, digital-store modeling, optimization, enterprise frontline execution, or governed agents.

Next decision: approve a bounded pilot and assign the owners who can validate data, access, licensing, security, and measurable success.
