SET Loop / role takeaway

One loop. The right view for each role.

The source does not get rebuilt six times. Each partner sees the part they can act on, at the right altitude, while the approved source, evidence, and decision rights remain intact.

Store

What applies here?

See: the exact week, launch, fixture, direction, and required action.

Do: execute, complete, submit proof, or raise one clear exception.

Return: actual timing, evidence, and practical store context.

Field

Where is support needed?

See: readiness, blockers, exceptions, and patterns across the district or region.

Do: coach, prioritize support, and route accountable questions.

Return: patterns, adoption friction, and targeted escalation.

Home Office

What is ready, blocked, or changing?

See: source lineage, scope, mapping, exceptions, evidence, and release state.

Do: build, govern, resolve, approve, and publish within owned authority.

Return: a clearer next launch built from store reality.

Store Team

Primary question: What do I need to do for my store today?

Receives: approved direction, simple fixture names, sequence, checklist, proof entry, and exception path.

Does not receive: unresolved content, source IDs, publishing controls, cross-store labor decisions, or Home Office exception administration.

SharePoint demo

Store Leadership

Primary question: What is the complete store workload, who owns it, and where do I need support?

Receives: store actions, timing scenario, accountable owners, linked direction, proof, and a support-request route.

Does not receive: source-file editing, enterprise publishing, payroll transfer, or final labor approval.

DM / RM

Primary question: Which stores are ready, blocked, or asking for the same support?

Receives: district or region readiness, exceptions, completion signals, store navigation, and focused support context.

Does not receive: unrestricted source data, Home Office publishing authority, or payroll action without accountable owners.

Field Leadership

Primary question: What pattern is affecting execution, adoption, or risk across the field?

Receives: summarized readiness, coverage, exception and adoption patterns, plus the controlled-pilot result.

Does not receive: source editing, publishing release, associate transfer, or a policy decision inside the demonstration.
